StreamHero — Quick Start & Instructions

🚀

1. Join or Create a Party

Enter your Nickname, pick an avatar, and enter a Room Code (default is MAIN). Share the same code with your friends to watch together.

📺

2. Stream Local Video or YouTube

Click Upload to drag & drop video files (.mp4, .webm, .mkv, .mov) or paste any YouTube link for synchronized streaming.

📱

3. Mobile & LAN QR Joining

Click Share QR in the top bar. Any phone or tablet on the same Wi-Fi can scan the QR code to join your stream instantly with zero setup.

🎧

4. Multi-Track & Dubbed Audio

Attach external dubbed audio files (.mp3, .m4a, .aac) from the audio selector to watch movies in your preferred language in sync.

⌨️ Keyboard & Gesture Shortcuts

Space or K Play / Pause
Double Click Seek ±10 Seconds
F11 or F Toggle Fullscreen
M Mute / Unmute
T Theater Mode
P Picture-in-Picture
